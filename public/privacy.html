Privacy notice

Effective October 26, 2026

Hururuk is a pre-launch project by a small student startup team in Korea. Right now we are only measuring interest. This notice explains the little data we collect.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you join the waitlist.
  • Which series page you were on when you joined, so we know which stories people want.
  • Anonymous usage events, such as which pages were opened and whether the subscribe button was clicked. These do not include your name, email or location, and we do not use cookies for them.

Why we collect it

To email you once when Hururuk launches, with your free first month, and to decide which series to license first. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Waitlist emails and anonymous usage events are stored by our hosting provider, Cloudflare. Cloudflare may store data outside your country. We do not share them with any analytics or advertising company.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist emails until three months after launch, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first. If we decide not to launch, we delete them.

Your choices

Joining the waitlist is optional. You can ask us to see or delete your email at any time by writing to kaimjy@kaist.ac.kr. We will reply within 14 days.
